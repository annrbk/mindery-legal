Request Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your Mindery account and all associated personal data, you can submit a request directly via email or through the mobile application settings.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your account profile and login credentials.
  • All recorded mood, emotion, and self-reflection tracking history.
  • Your app preferences, settings, and personal statistics.

Note: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once processed. Processing may take up to 30 days.
