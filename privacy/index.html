1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Mindery mobile application ("Application") for Android devices. Mindery is a personal mood-tracking service designed to help users process and record their emotional states.

2. Information We Collect and Purpose of Processing

We collect only the minimum necessary data to provide and improve our services:

Data Category Specific Data Points Purpose of Processing
Account Information Name, Email address Account creation, authentication, transactional notifications.
Security Credentials Passwords (stored in hashed format only) Account protection and authentication.
Personal & Sensitive Content Mood tracking logs, timestamps, emotion records Core application functionality (providing mood insights).
Technical & Device Data IP address, Operating System version, crash logs, diagnostic data Application performance, stability, and troubleshooting.

3. Third-Party Software Development Kits (SDKs) and Services

We partner with third-party service providers to support application infrastructure, analytics, and messaging. These third parties process data under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: App infrastructure, authentication, and platform features.
  • Expo: Framework execution and update delivery.
  • Resend: Direct delivery of transactional emails (e.g., account verification, password resets).

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, our legal basis for processing your personal data includes:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing Account Information and Personal Content to provide the Mindery service.
  • Explicit Consent: Processing sensitive data (mood logs) based on your explicit consent granted during account registration.
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing Technical & Device Data to maintain app stability and security.

5. User Rights and Account Deletion Policy

You have the right to access, rectify, export, or request the permanent deletion of your personal data.

Account & Data Deletion Options:

  • In-App: You can delete your account and associated mood logs directly inside the Application via Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Email Request: You may contact us at support.mindery@gmail.com to request manual data deletion.

Upon processing an account deletion request, all personal content, account records, and mood logs will be permanently wiped from our active databases within 30 days.

6. Data Retention Policy

  • User Data & Mood Logs: Retained as long as your account remains active. Upon account deletion, all personal data is deleted.
  • Technical & System Logs: Retained for a maximum period of 24 months for security audit and performance analysis purposes.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete it from our servers.

8. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical safeguards—including database encryption in transit and at rest—to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of your personal data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be notified within the application or via email prior to becoming effective.
